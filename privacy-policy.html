VaultLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

VaultLog is a training log for pole vaulters, made by Adam Pantzar. This page explains what data the app stores, why, and how to delete it.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and password. The password is stored only as a secure hash; nobody can read it.
  • Training data you enter: sessions (date, location, indoor/outdoor, wind, notes), vaults (bar height, result, pole, steps, grip, run-up, standards, notes) and poles (name, brand, length, weight, flex).
  • Preferences: units and run-up measuring method.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, a technical report (the error, app version, device model, operating system and a random ID for this installation of the app) is sent so the problem can be fixed. It does not include your email, training data or IP address.

Vaults logged without an internet connection are kept on your phone until they can be uploaded.

Why

Only to run the app: to sign you in, keep your training log in sync across your devices, show your statistics, and fix bugs. There are no ads, no tracking for advertising, and your data is never sold.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores your account and training data (in the EU, Ireland).
  • Sentry receives crash reports (stored in the EU, Germany).
  • Resend sends account emails (confirmation and password-reset codes). Resend is based in the USA; it only receives your email address and the code, under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your training data is private: only you can see it. It is never shared with other users or third parties beyond the services above.

How long

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, all of it is deleted immediately and permanently.

Deleting your account

In the app: Home → Settings (⚙) → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account together with all your sessions, vaults, poles and preferences.

Without the app: email contact@vaultlog.co from the address you signed up with and ask for your VaultLog account to be deleted. It will be deleted, with all its data, within 30 days.

Your rights

You can see and change your data in the app at any time, and delete it as described above. For any other request (for example a copy of your data), email contact@vaultlog.co.

Children

VaultLog is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
